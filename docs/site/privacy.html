Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Playnite Link is a local independent extension. There is no Playnite Link account server, central save database, analytics service or advertising.

Shared data

Your selected vault holds chosen save backups, game identifiers and metadata, playtime sessions, device records and optional extension/theme installation files. Devices connected to that vault can read shared data. Choose a private vault and trusted devices.

Local credentials and settings

Provider authorization uses Rclone. Tokens and configuration remain local. Playnite settings, extension settings, sign-ins, active theme and optional passcode remain device-specific. The passcode is a local app lock; it does not encrypt the vault.

Permissions and online services

Current Google Drive authorization requests broad Drive access. Extension operations target the configured vault, but permission is broader than one folder. Inspect or revoke access in your provider account. Optional HowLongToBeat uploads update existing mapped entries using its extension’s local sign-in. Provider authorization and runtime or addon downloads contact their respective services.

This website

These static pages receive no saves, OAuth tokens or passcodes. Netlify serves the pages and may process ordinary request information under its privacy policy. No application backend or analytics scripts are installed here.

Your control

Disable sync, disconnect a device or revoke authorization to stop access. Disabling sync does not erase local backups or cloud files. For support, open an issue; never include credentials, private saves or configuration.
